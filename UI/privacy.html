PRIVACY

Your footage
stays yours.

Plain-language summary of where your data goes. Last updated 2026-10-27.

On your computer

  • Your source video files. Prism copies them into a project and never changes the originals.
  • Projects, previews and rendered MP4s.

Sent for AI processing

Prism has no offline mode. On Mac, requests go through Prism’s server (Supabase) to OpenRouter and an AI model provider. Windows and Linux send requests directly to Google’s Gemini API using your own API key. The relevant providers’ terms apply to this data:

  • Audio chunks of the part of the video you selected.
  • Transcripts.
  • Sampled frames: up to 12 per refinement batch, and about 1 per second of each tracked Short.
  • Editor chat messages, with recent conversation and a short edit summary.

Temporary audio files are deleted after each request. The Mac server logs may include portions of model responses for diagnostics. See OpenRouter’s Privacy Policy and Terms, and Google’s Privacy Policy and Gemini API terms.

Your Prism account

Your Prism account is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Paid plans are not available during the current beta. The account stores:

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored hashed by Supabase Auth.
  • Any Paddle subscription and customer IDs, status and next billing date, if associated with your account.
  • One record per finished export from the Mac app: the date, number of Shorts, total length in seconds and export quality.
  • One record per video you make Shorts from: a fingerprint of the file’s name and length (not the video), the day and its length in minutes.
  • The total cost of your AI requests and any promo codes you redeemed, to enforce your allowance.

No video, audio, transcripts or titles are stored in your account. Database rules let each user read only their own records. Deleting your account deletes its export records.

Questions or deletion requests

Open an issue on GitHub to ask a question or to have your account deleted.
