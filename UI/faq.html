Everything to know before your first Short.

How much does Prism cost?

Prism is in beta and paid plans are not available yet. AI usage limits apply; check Settings in the app for your allowance. About beta access.

What happens to my footage?

Your source files, projects, preview, and rendering stay on your computer. Audio chunks, transcripts, sampled frames, and editor chat messages are sent for AI processing. On Mac, they go through Prism’s server and OpenRouter to an AI model; on Windows and Linux, they go to Google Gemini. Prism needs an internet connection; it has no offline AI mode.

Can I change the Shorts Prism picks?

Yes. Review and adjust every Short before export. Change the timing, choose Fill, Fit, or Split framing, try seven caption styles, or ask the editor chat to help with edits.

Where can I post my exports?

Prism exports vertical MP4 files at up to 1080 × 1920. Upload them yourself to your favorite short-form platforms, including YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. Direct publishing isn’t built in.

Is this ready for my daily workflow?

Prism is an early beta. The Mac pipeline works end to end, but you should review every clip and expect rough edges. Windows and Linux ports are available but still need verification on real devices. Read the current limits.

Do I need a Prism account?

On Mac, sign in to the app with your Prism account to use the AI service. Your dashboard also shows your export stats and history. Windows and Linux use your own Gemini API key instead. Create an account.

How do I get started?

Download Prism, sign in on Mac or add your Gemini API key on Windows or Linux, and import a video. See the three-step workflow for a quick tour.
